### 💎 What is Crystade?

Every engineering team eventually wrestles with fragmented operations: a forgotten crontab on an EC2 instance, silent script failures, disjointed uptime checkers, and a third-party status page that costs a fortune just to alert subscribers.

**Crystade** replaces that chaos with a single, collaborative platform. We combine managed cron jobs, deep multi-protocol health monitoring, structured incident response, and hosted status pages under one clean roof.

- ⏰ **Zero-Infra Managed Cron Jobs:** Execute outbound HTTP schedules with full execution snapshots (request, response, timing, and scheduler lag). No VPS drift, no in-app scheduler headaches.
- 📡 **Multi-Protocol Active Monitoring:** Proactively probe your systems globally across HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2, TCP, TLS certificate expiration, and custom game protocols like Minecraft.
- 🚦 **Integrated Incidents & Status Pages:** Monitor-detected failures automatically turn into incident timelines and publish directly to branded status pages (`your-team.crystade.io`) with zero manual bridging.
- 🤝 **Built for Teams First:** Unlimited seats across all plans, role-based controls, team-scoped resources, and shared audit trails.
